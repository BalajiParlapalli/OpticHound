## 4. Nature of Surveillance Detection & Realistic Scope (No Misleading Claims)

OpticHound is an intelligent heuristic diagnostic scanner designed to assist users in detecting network-connected surveillance equipment. You explicitly acknowledge the following technical boundaries:

- **Network-Connected Cameras Only**: OpticHound scans for devices transmitting data via Wi-Fi, Ethernet, RTSP, ONVIF, UPnP, or ambient wireless beaconing.
- **Offline / Non-Transmitting Devices**: Standalone hidden cameras that do not connect to Wi-Fi and record exclusively to internal microSD cards without transmitting cannot be detected via RF/network scans. This is why OpticHound provides an included **Physical Inspection Checklist** (flashlight lens reflection and night-vision infrared inspection).
- **Heuristic Scoring**: Risk scores are heuristic estimates based on port fingerprints and manufacturer signatures. A low score does not constitute a certified legal warranty that a premises is completely free of all surveillance mechanisms, nor does a high score guarantee an illegal camera without physical verification.
