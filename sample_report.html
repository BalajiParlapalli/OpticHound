🕵️ Traveler's Physical Inspection Guide

Even hidden cameras with hidden SSIDs or offline memory cards leave physical clues. Perform these checks in bedrooms and bathrooms.

⏰ Bedside Alarm Clocks

Modern hidden cameras are often housed inside functional digital alarm clocks facing the bed. Inspect the tinted acrylic face with a direct flashlight beam to spot circular lenses.

🔌 USB Chargers & Adapters

Wall charger cubes plugged into outlets near the bed or bathroom frequently conceal pinhole cameras. Unplug suspect wall adapters and inspect the front face for a pinhole aperture.

🚨 Smoke & CO Detectors

Examine ceiling smoke detectors directly over the bed or shower. Pinhole cameras require an unobstructed hole that often differs from standard alarm vents.

🔦 Phone Flashlight Reflection

Turn off room lights. Shine your smartphone flashlight at wall art, screw heads, AC vents, and mirror frames. Camera lenses will produce a distinctive purple, green, or blue reflection.

📱 Infrared Night Vision Test

Darken the room completely. Open your phone camera (or selfie camera) and look around. Night-vision infrared LEDs emit a glowing violet or bright white dot on camera sensors.

🕶️ Smart Glasses & Camera Frames

Inspect sunglasses or spectacles left on tables, bedside shelves, or charging docks. Look for unusually thick temple arms (housing battery/electronics), front corner pinhole camera lenses adjacent to frame hinges, and white privacy notification LEDs.

📸 If You Spot a Camera

Do NOT tamper with or disconnect the camera. Photograph the device and surrounding room context, leave the rental immediately, and contact Airbnb/booking customer support and local police.
