# Cookie Policy for OpticHound

**Effective Date:** October 25, 2026  
**Last Updated:** October 25, 2026  
**Tool Name:** OpticHound (Surveillance Audit & Network Security Tool)

---

## 1. Zero Cookies Declaration

**OpticHound does NOT use cookies of any kind.**

When you run OpticHound or open its generated HTML audit report (`.html`), the software operates entirely on your local machine without creating, reading, or storing:
- No first-party HTTP cookies
- No third-party tracking or advertising cookies
- No session or authentication cookies
- No persistent local tracking beacons or web pixels
- No cross-site identifier storage

---

## 2. Why There is No Annoying Cookie Banner

Under international privacy regulations—including the **EU ePrivacy Directive (Directive 2002/58/EC as amended by 2009/136/EC)**, the **EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)**, and India's **Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act)**:

- Cookie consent banners are legally mandated **only** when a website or service stores or retrieves information from a user's terminal equipment for non-essential tracking, advertising, analytics, or profiling purposes.
- Because OpticHound is an offline, self-contained HTML document that uses **zero tracking mechanisms**, there is no tracking to consent to. 
- Rather than displaying an intrusive or deceptive "cookie consent banner", OpticHound protects user privacy by eliminating cookies altogether at the code level.

---

## 3. Local Technical State in the HTML Report

The generated HTML report includes interactive controls (such as filtering devices by risk level, expanding cards, and searching by IP address). 
- These interactions are processed purely **in client-side RAM using standard browser JavaScript variables**.
- No preference cookies or analytics records are written to your hard drive or sent across the internet.
- When you close your browser tab, the temporary JavaScript state in memory is immediately cleared.

---

## 4. Updates to this Policy

Because OpticHound is committed to zero tracking, we do not anticipate adding cookies or tracking mechanisms in the future. Any updates to this policy will be documented in the official repository documentation.
